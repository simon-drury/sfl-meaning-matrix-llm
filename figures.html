These figures present the central representational object of the proposal: the composition of M0 from incoming language, structured meaning-state configurations, their movement across a meaning trajectory, computation over that trajectory, and boundary realisation into readable language.

Pipeline order: Figure 0, Figure 1, Figure 2, Figure 4, Figure 3.

Figure 0. SFL-Native Preprocessing: Composition of M0

Incoming language analysed along metafunctional organisation and contextual configuration, composing the initial meaning-state matrix, with lexis parked for boundary realisation.

Figure 1. Meaning-State Transition: M0 to M1

Two separate meaning-state matrices with directed vectors running cell to cell, presenting a single delta between successive configurations.

Figure 2. Meaning Trajectory: M0 to M3

Four successive meaning-state matrices with sequential deltas, presenting the trajectory as the unit over which computation and boundary realisation are investigated.

Figure 3. Boundary Realisation: Mn to Working-Language Text

A final meaning configuration realised directly in English and Spanish as co-equal realisations, with the recorded outputs of the iconic prompts.

Figure 4. Computation over the Meaning Trajectory

A causal model over 9D meaning states predicting the next state from preceding states. Candidate formulation: code on an unmerged branch, with no training result claimed.
