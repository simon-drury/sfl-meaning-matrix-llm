Architecture: SFL-native preprocessing

SFL-Native Preprocessing: Composition of M0

Incoming language enters the SFL-native preprocessing environment, which analyses it along ideational, interpersonal, and textual metafunctions and relative to field, tenor, and mode as contextual configuration. The resulting values compose the initial meaning-state matrix M0. Lexical items are parked outside the matrix and remain available to boundary realisation. The matrix carries meaning-making values; it carries no lexical units.

Repository implementation evidence

  • sfl_matrix_engine.py: composes M0 for the iconic prompts and holds lexis outside the trajectory.
  • sfl_matrix_engine_v3.py: later matrix-engine revision.
  • ingest_corpus.py: corpus ingestion into meaning-state trajectories.
