Architecture: boundary realisation

Boundary Realisation: Mn to Working-Language Text

Boundary realisation produces readable language from the final meaning configuration of a trajectory. The same meaning state is realised directly in whichever language is the working language. Each realisation is primary: the English and Spanish outputs are co-equal realisations of one meaning configuration, and neither is derived from the other.

Repository implementation evidence

  • sfl_matrix_engine.py: encodes the iconic EN and ES prompts and applies the minimal realisation rule.
  • sfl_realize.py: realisation prototype.
  • interact.py: realisation pipeline with empirical vocabulary anchors.
