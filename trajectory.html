Architecture: meaning trajectory

Meaning Trajectory: M0, M1, M2, M3

A meaning trajectory is the ordered sequence of meaning-state configurations produced as incoming language is mapped into the SFL-native representation. Each successive state differs from its predecessor by a delta. The trajectory, rather than any single state, is the unit over which the research programme examines computation and boundary realisation.

Repository implementation evidence

  • sfl_matrix_engine.py: maps prompts to M0 and successive deltas.
  • sfl_manifold.py: computes trajectory geometry over successive states.
  • sfl_animate.py: existing trajectory animation prototype.
